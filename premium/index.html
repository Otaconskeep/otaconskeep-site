License & sharing protocol

One seat. One member. Clear rules.

  1. Issuance: You pay on Buy Me a Coffee → Antonio records payment → issues access name + password + PIN + License ID → registers the seat here.
  2. Seat limit: Default is 1 seat (one primary machine/household). Extra seats are issued deliberately, not implied.
  3. Unlock signal: Each successful unlock can notify Antonio with License ID, IP, timezone, and browser fingerprint. Repeated unlocks from unrelated places look like sharing.
  4. If credentials are shared: Seat may be suspended or revoked. Credentials are rotated. The original payer is contacted on Discord when possible.
  5. If you lose access or suspect a leak: Message Antonio in Discord with your License ID. Do not post passwords in public channels.
  6. Fair use: Teaching a friend is fine; handing them your password is not. They can support and get their own seat.

Antonio checks seats and payment refs in the operator desk. Members only see their own card above.
