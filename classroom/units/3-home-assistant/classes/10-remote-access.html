Remote access ≠ randomly opening ports. Choose VPN vs authenticated tunnel/proxy with a decision chart.

In plain words: Don’t hang Sonarr and qBit on the public internet just because you learned port forwarding.
More detail: LAN/WAN/NAT/firewall/port-forward/reverse-proxy/VPN/tunnel/TLS/DNS — pick the smallest door that solves the need.
