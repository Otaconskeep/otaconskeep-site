# Quiz: SABnzbd and Usenet Downloading

**Module:** Download Clients & Indexers
**Activity type:** Quiz / retrieval practice
**Target:** ≥80%
**Objective:** Describe the roles of a Usenet provider, an indexer, an NZB file, and SABnzbd.

## Questions

1. 1. What does an NZB file primarily contain: downloaded payload data, article references and metadata, provider credentials, or PAR2 recovery blocks?
2. 2. Why should SABnzbd use separate incomplete and completed directories?
3. 3. What is the difference between a Usenet indexer and a Usenet provider?
4. 4. Why does high provider retention not guarantee that a job will complete?
5. 5. What problem can PAR2 solve, and what is its main limitation?
6. 6. Why should a backup provider normally have a lower priority than the primary provider?
7. 7. Name two controls that reduce risk around the SABnzbd management interface.
8. 8. Why should post-processing scripts be treated as high-trust components?
9. 9. Does successful verification prove that completed content is safe and legally authorized?
10. 10. What setting must an operator check before increasing the number of provider connections?
